About our campus

A campus in the mountains, and a breeze that carries every conversation.

IIT Mandi is one of the eight newer IITs, set up in 2009 in the Kamand Valley of Mandi district, Himachal Pradesh. The residential campus spreads across about 538 acres beside the Uhl river, roughly 14 km from Mandi town, and is made up of a South and a North campus. The institute is also home to IIT Mandi Catalyst, the first technology business incubator in Himachal Pradesh.

Zephyr asks a simple question: how does a community this spread out among the pines stay close? Our answer is one place for hostels, clubs, study groups and late-night ideas.

Features

Everything a campus conversation needs.

The basics work flawlessly, and then we added the things we wished our own group chats had.

Campus rooms

Six rooms ready on day one: Commons, Hostel Hub, Clubs, Lost & Found, Placements and Study Hall.

Direct and group chats

Private one-to-one conversations and groups, delivered instantly over WebSockets.

Shared focus timers

Type /focus 25 and everyone in the room studies against the same live countdown.

Sumit AI assistant

Ask questions with @sumit or summarize a long thread with one click.

Voice notes and files

Record voice notes, share images, PDFs and documents without leaving the chat.

Live signals

Typing indicators, read receipts, online status, reactions and unread badges.

Private by design

Messages are encrypted at rest, and sessions use secure httpOnly cookies.

Safe uploads

Every file is checked for type, content and unsafe markup before anyone can open it.
